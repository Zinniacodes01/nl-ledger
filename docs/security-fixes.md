Two of two reports downloaded and the generated index was read back; `pdftotext` identified Davis in both. `DavisBernardSum2025-26.pdf`: 91,808 bytes, SHA-256 `76a9ece36a8a2a362539e262f837682cf8ddf3ab5dfe8d7a8ec5eec11bbcee8e`. `DavisBernardDet2025-26.pdf`: 343,456 bytes, SHA-256 `dc5c05698e28514cc2bea74700866058ab7a77ec8c2707c337f5ea982f5b2062`. - Real cached CanadaBuys CSV re-parsed with output captured instead of written: 535 selected notices, CAD/USD/unstated, original currency evidence retained for every notice.

## Deployment proof still required

After an authorized deployment, read back the Worker's settings and issue one synthetic receipt request. Verify the maintained redaction survives deployment and only that synthetic request's logged URL omits its query. Do not inspect other visitors' incomes. Confirm equivalent ordinary share URLs reuse the edge cache, the render budget protects misses, and normal cards stay below the 1,000 ms ceiling on Cloudflare.

Cloudflare documents the [supported config redaction field](https://developers.cloudflare.com/cf/projects/config-explorer/), [eventually consistent rate limiting](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/runtime-apis/bindings/rate-limit/), and [CPU enforcement](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/platform/limits/). [Web Analytics currently does not log query strings](https://developers.cloudflare.com/web-analytics/faq/); no analytics settings or third-party beacon were changed. This redaction does not erase old logs, browser history, voluntarily shared links or an income a visitor writes into the free-text note.
